# Shipping Policy — Haven & Hearth

## UK standard delivery
Most small and medium items (home textiles, kitchenware, small decor) ship with
standard delivery in **2–3 working days**. Standard delivery is **free on orders
over £50**, otherwise **£3.95**.

Orders placed **before 2pm on a working day** are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed after 2pm, or at the weekend, are dispatched the next working day.
Orders placed on a UK bank holiday are dispatched the following working day.

## UK large-item delivery (furniture)
Large furniture (sofas, bed frames, dining tables, wardrobes) is **not** shipped
by standard courier. It is delivered by a two-person team on a scheduled day,
typically **2–4 weeks** from order. You will be contacted to book a delivery
slot. Large-item delivery is **kerbside only** and costs a **flat £29** — the
standard free-over-£50 threshold does **not** apply to furniture. If you need
delivery into the room, or disassembly/removal of an old item, select the
"room-of-choice" service at checkout for an additional fee.

## European Union delivery
We ship to the following EU destinations: Ireland, France, Germany, the
Netherlands, Belgium, Spain, Portugal and Italy.

EU delivery takes **5–10 working days** and costs a **flat £9.95**, free on
orders over **£120**. The £50 UK free-shipping threshold does not apply to EU
orders.

**Customs and import duties:** EU orders are shipped **delivery duty unpaid
(DDU)**. This means any customs charges, import VAT, or handling fees applied by
the destination country's customs authority are the **customer's
responsibility** and are not included in the price shown at checkout. Haven &
Hearth does not collect or estimate these charges. If you refuse delivery
because of customs fees, the return shipping and any duties remain your cost
and will be deducted from your refund.

## Tracking and signatures
Every order is dispatched with a tracking number, emailed to you as soon as the
label is generated. High-value orders (over £500) require a **signature on
delivery**; if no one is available to sign, the parcel is returned to the local
depot for redelivery.

## International (non-EU)
We currently ship only to the UK and the EU destinations listed above. We do
not ship to the United States, Canada, or elsewhere outside the EU.
