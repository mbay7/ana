# Accounts & Privacy Policy — Haven & Hearth

## Creating and using an account
You do **not** need an account to shop — guest checkout is available. Creating
an account (just an email and password) gives you order history, saved
addresses, faster checkout, and a place to manage returns. Accounts are free.

## Order history and saved details
Once logged in, you can view your full order history, download VAT invoices,
track open orders, and manage saved delivery addresses. You can save a card for
faster checkout; the card details are stored as a **token** by our payment
provider, never as a full card number in our systems.

## Closing your account
To close your account, contact support at help@havenandhearth.example. We will
close it within **30 days**. Any open orders or pending returns are processed
first; closing your account does not cancel in-flight orders.

## Your data and GDPR (UK)
We are the data controller for your personal data. Your rights include:

- **Right of access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold.
- **Right to rectification** — correct inaccurate data.
- **Right to erasure** ("right to be forgotten") — ask us to delete your data
  unless we have a legal reason (such as tax records) to keep it.
- **Right to data portability** — receive your data in a machine-readable
  format.
- **Right to restrict processing** and **right to object** to direct marketing.

We retain order records for **6 years** to meet tax and accounting obligations,
then delete or anonymise them. We delete marketing-list data as soon as you
unsubscribe.

## Marketing
We only send marketing email if you **opt in**. Every marketing email has an
unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing is immediate. Transactional emails (order
confirmations, dispatch notices, delivery updates) are always sent regardless
of your marketing preference, because they are required to fulfil your order.

## Security
Your account data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Passwords are
stored as salted hashes, never in plain text. We will never ask for your
password by email; genuine emails from us link to our own site rather than
asking you to reply with details.
