# Frequently Asked Questions — Haven & Hearth

**Q: How long does UK standard delivery take?**
A: Standard UK delivery takes 2–3 working days, and it's free on orders over £50
(£3.95 otherwise).

**Q: Do you deliver to the EU?**
A: Yes — to Ireland, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Belgium, Spain, Portugal
and Italy. EU delivery takes 5–10 working days, costs £9.95, and is free over
£120.

**Q: Do I have to pay customs on an EU order?**
A: Possibly. EU orders are shipped delivery duty unpaid, so any import duties,
VAT or handling fees charged by your country's customs are your responsibility.

**Q: How much is furniture delivery?**
A: Large furniture is delivered by a two-person team at a flat £29 (kerbside),
and it is not covered by the free-over-£50 offer.

**Q: How long does furniture take to arrive?**
A: Furniture is usually delivered within 2–4 weeks, and you'll be contacted to
book a delivery day.

**Q: Can I return something I just changed my mind about?**
A: Yes, for most items you have 30 days to return an item that is unused and in
its original packaging. You pay the return postage.

**Q: What's the difference between the 14-day and 30-day return windows?**
A: The 14-day window is your statutory cancellation right under UK law — if you
cancel within 14 days you get a full refund including standard delivery. The
30-day window is our more generous change-of-mind policy, where you still pay
return postage.

**Q: Do you refund the delivery charge when I cancel?**
A: Only if you cancel within the 14-day statutory window. Change-of-mind returns
under the 30-day policy do not include a refund of delivery.

**Q: My item arrived broken. Do I pay to send it back?**
A: No. Faulty, damaged or misdescribed items are returned free and you get a
full refund or a replacement.

**Q: Can I return a personalised item?**
A: No — personalised or made-to-order items are final sale once production
begins, unless the item is faulty or not what you ordered.

**Q: Can I return bedding once I've opened it?**
A: No, not for change of mind. Hygiene-sealed items (mattresses, toppers,
pillows, duvets) cannot be returned once the seal is broken, for health reasons.

**Q: When do seasonal items become non-returnable?**
A: The Christmas collection becomes final sale from 1 December, and the summer
garden collection from 1 September.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**
A: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay and Klarna.

**Q: When is my card charged?**
A: At checkout, except for pre-orders and backorders, which are charged when the
item is dispatched.

**Q: Can I pay in instalments?**
A: Yes, with Klarna you can split the cost into three interest-free instalments
or pay later, subject to Klarna's credit check.

**Q: Are gift cards refundable?**
A: No — gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged for cash.

**Q: Do I need an account to order?**
A: No, you can check out as a guest. An account gives you order history and
faster checkout.

**Q: When do I get a tracking number?**
A: A tracking number is emailed to you as soon as your order is dispatched, not
while it's still processing.

**Q: My tracking hasn't moved for days. What do I do?**
A: For UK orders, contact us if there's no movement for more than 3 working
days. We treat a parcel as lost after 10 working days.

**Q: Do I have to sign for my order?**
A: Only for high-value orders over £500, which require a signature.

**Q: How do I close my account?**
A: Contact support and we'll close it within 30 days. This doesn't cancel any
in-flight orders.

**Q: How long do you keep my data?**
A: We keep order records for 6 years to meet tax obligations, then delete or
anonymise them.

**Q: How long do I have to claim for a faulty item?**
A: Up to six months under the Consumer Rights Act, and longer for latent
manufacturing defects.

**Q: What's your warranty on electricals?**
A: Small electricals carry a 2-year warranty against manufacturing defects,
starting from delivery.

**Q: Can I change my delivery address after ordering?**
A: Contact support as soon as possible. Once an order is dispatched we cannot
redirect it.

**Q: What happens if my payment is declined?**
A: Your order is placed on hold, we retry once, and if it still fails the order
may be cancelled after 7 days.

**Q: Do you do price matching?**
A: No, we don't offer price-matching.

**Q: Is furniture assembly included?**
A: Not by default. Furniture arrives flat-packed; self-assembly is required
unless you add the assembly service at checkout.
