# Returns & Refunds Policy — Haven & Hearth

## Our 30-day change-of-mind returns
For most items, you have **30 days** from the date of delivery to return an item
you no longer want. The item must be **unused**, in its **original packaging**,
with tags still attached. To start a return, generate a returns label from the
"Orders" section of your account. For a change-of-mind return, **you pay the
return postage**. Some larger items may also incur a restocking fee of up to
10%, stated on the returns form.

## Your statutory 14-day cancellation right (UK)
Under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, you also have a separate,
**statutory right to cancel** any online order within **14 days** of receiving
it, for any reason. Unlike our 30-day policy, if you cancel within this 14-day
window you are entitled to a **full refund including the standard delivery
charge**. You may inspect the goods as you would in a shop, but you are liable
for any reduction in value caused by unnecessary handling. You must tell us you
are canceling within 14 days (a returns form or a clear written message is
enough), then return the item within a further 14 days.

The 14-day statutory right and the 30-day store policy are **different things**.
The 14-day right guarantees the return of the standard delivery charge; the
30-day policy is a more generous change-of-mind window where you still pay
return postage.

## Faulty, damaged or misdescribed items
If an item is **faulty, damaged in transit, or not as described**, you are
entitled to a **full refund or a replacement**, and we **pay the return
shipping**. These are your statutory rights and they apply for a longer period
(up to six months under the Consumer Rights Act, and longer if a fault is a
latent manufacturing defect). This is separate from change-of-mind returns: a
faulty item is returned free, whereas a change-of-mind item is returned at your
cost.

## Items that cannot be returned (final sale / non-returnable)
The following cannot be returned for a change of mind:

- **Personalised or made-to-order items** (engraving, monogramming, custom
  sizing) — these are final sale once production begins.
- **Hygiene-sealed items once opened** (mattress toppers, pillows, duvets,
  bedding where the seal is broken) — for health reasons.
- **Perishable goods.**
- **Clearance items explicitly marked "final sale"** at the time of purchase.

These exclusions apply to change-of-mind returns only. If a final-sale or
non-returnable item is faulty or not as described, your statutory rights to a
refund or replacement still apply.

## Seasonal collections
Our seasonal ranges — the **Christmas collection** and the **summer garden
collection** — follow special rules. After the cut-off date for each season,
unsold seasonal items are moved to final sale and become **non-returnable**.
The Christmas collection becomes final sale from **1 December** each year; the
summer garden collection from **1 September**.

## How refunds are issued
Once we receive and inspect your return, a refund is issued to your **original
payment method within 14 days**. If you paid with Klarna, the refund is handled
through Klarna in line with its own schedule.
