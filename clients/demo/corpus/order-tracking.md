# Order Tracking & Delivery Status — Haven & Hearth

## When you get a tracking number
A **tracking number is emailed to you as soon as your order is dispatched**,
along with a link to the carrier's tracking page. You will not receive a
tracking number while an order is still being prepared.

## Order statuses explained
- **Processing** — your order has been received and payment confirmed; it is
  being picked and packed. Standard orders leave this stage within 24 hours.
- **Shipped / Dispatched** — your order has left our warehouse and a tracking
  number has been generated.
- **Out for delivery** — the parcel is with the courier for delivery today.
- **Delivered** — the parcel has been delivered; check the tracking page for a
  delivery photo or signature.

## Large-item delivery (furniture)
Furniture orders do **not** receive a standard courier tracking number. Instead,
you are contacted to book a delivery slot, and you receive a confirmation of the
scheduled day and a two-hour window by email and SMS.

## EU economy shipping
Some EU orders are dispatched on an economy service that has **reduced or
no tracking** after the parcel leaves the UK. If your EU order shows no tracking
updates for more than **5 working days**, contact support so we can open an
investigation with the carrier.

## Missing or stalled tracking
If a UK tracking number shows **no movement for more than 3 working days**,
contact support. We treat a parcel as lost after **10 working days** with no
update; at that point we will send a replacement or issue a refund.

## Missed deliveries
If a courier attempts delivery and no one is home, the parcel is returned to the
local depot and redelivery is arranged automatically. After **two failed
attempts**, the parcel is returned to us; we will contact you to arrange
re-delivery or issue a refund (minus any return shipping we incurred).
High-value items (over £500) require a signature and will **not** be left in a
safe place.
