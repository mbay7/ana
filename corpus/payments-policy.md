# Payments Policy — Haven & Hearth

## Accepted payment methods
We accept **Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay,
and Klarna** (Pay in 3 instalments or Pay Later, subject to a credit check).
All card payments are processed securely; we do not store full card numbers
ourselves — payment details are tokenised by our payment provider and are
PCI-compliant.

## When your card is charged
For most items, your card is charged in full **at checkout**. The exception is
**pre-orders and backordered items**: for these, we take an authorisation (a
temporary hold) at checkout but only **capture the payment when the item is
dispatched**. You will see the temporary hold appear and then drop off if the
item is cancelled or delayed beyond 30 days.

## Klarna
Klarna lets you split the cost into three interest-free instalments or pay
later. Klarna runs its own credit and affordability checks, and the decision is
Klarna's, not ours. If you miss a Klarna payment, Klarna will contact you
directly and may charge late fees; Haven & Hearth has no role in that process.
If you return an item paid for with Klarna, the refund is processed through
Klarna and applied to your instalment plan.

## Failed or declined payments
If a payment is declined, your order is placed **on hold** while we attempt to
contact you. We will retry the payment once automatically; if it fails again,
the order may be cancelled after 7 days. You will always be notified before
cancellation.

## Gift cards and vouchers
- Gift cards are **non-refundable** and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
- E-gift cards are delivered by email within one hour of purchase.
- Promotional vouchers apply at checkout and cannot be applied after an order
  is placed, nor combined with another promo unless stated.

## Prices and discounts
Prices are shown in GBP including VAT where applicable. We do **not** offer
price-matching. Sale prices apply only while the promotion is live; we do not
retrospectively refund the difference if an item is later discounted.

## Refunds
All refunds go back to the **original payment method**. Where we cannot refund
to the original method (for example, a card that has closed), we will contact
you to arrange an alternative, such as a refund to a different card or a gift
card credit.
