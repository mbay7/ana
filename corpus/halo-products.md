# HALO — Product Range

HALO is a full-face glow system, priced for "affordable aspiration" (below
Rhode, Merit and Summer Fridays; closer to Bubble and Byoma).

## Prep & Protect
- **GLOW SPF** (50ml, $30–35) — daily sunscreen with a dewy, luminous finish.
  Protect + glow in one step. Use every day before makeup.
- **GLOW MILK** (40ml, $25–30) — the first layer of glaze. A lightweight
  makeup-prep milk that hydrates, supports the skin barrier, improves makeup
  wear, and gives that first glazed base. Ingredients: ceramides (strengthen),
  niacinamide (brighten), hyaluronic acid (hydrate). "Milk before makeup."

## Face
- **SKIN TINT** (40ml, $28–30) — a lightweight skin enhancer, not heavy
  foundation. Clean, reflective, easy, sheer. For an even, natural glow.
- **CREAM BLUSH** (5.3ml, $20–25) — soft fresh color with a skin-like finish.
  "Cheeks, but make it cloud."
- **CREAM BRONZER** (5.3ml, $20–25) — natural warmth, never a harsh contour.
- **LIQUID HIGHLIGHTER** (12ml, $24–28) — light reflection and glazed
  dimension. Two swipes, zero effort, all shine.

## Lips
- **TREATMENT LIP TINT** ($17–18) — color with lip care. Hydrating, sheer and
  buildable, non-sticky. Six shades: Milk Pink, Petal, Rosé, Fig, Berry, Coco.
- **LIP STAIN LINER** ($19) — a tattoo-soft liner that defines lips and stays,
  with skincare benefits. "Tattoo-soft. Zero fuss."

## Eyes
- **EYE GLAZE** (5–8ml, $19–20) — soft shimmer in 1–2 shades, for an easy
  reflective, glazed eye.
- **ESSENTIAL CLEAN GIRL QUAD** (4 × 1.5ml, $24) — four minimal shades that are
  exactly the colors needed for the clean girl look, no excess.

## Color system
Two worlds, one Halo. Baby Blue for skin, Pistachio for color; one glazed,
reflective finish across both. Brand palette: Mist Sage, Water Blue, Cloud
Grey, Pearl White (inspired by light, water, reflection).
