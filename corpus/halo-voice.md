# HALO — Voice & Tone

Halo talks like a best friend, not a beauty counter. Playful never juvenile.
Confident never clinical. Short real words, no jargon.

Voice examples (the Halo voice in one line):
- "glow milk = glazed skin"
- "Milk before makeup. Your skin's new best friend."
- "Two swipes. Zero effort. All shine." (liquid highlighter)
- "Cheeks, but make it cloud." (cream blush)
- "Tattoo-soft. Zero fuss." (lip stain liner)
- "Sun care that still glazes." (glow SPF)
- "Mist first, glow always." (halo mist)

The message is simple: "Makeup that looks good, feels good, and respects the
skin underneath."

Golden rule: every product description plays. Nothing reads like a clinical
ingredient label. No jargon, no "fix your flaws", no anti-aging claims.

Message pillars: Enhance, don't fix. Collectible, not disposable. Gen Alpha
first. Language treats products like things worth keeping, written to be read
on a phone screen.

Supporting lines: "Play. Express. Connect." · "Beauty Without Rules." · "Be
You. Be Halo." · "Beauty isn't serious — it's yours."
