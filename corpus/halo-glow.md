# HALO — The Glow System

The whole brand is built around ONE idea: glazed skin. Not one product — a
connected finish across the full face. Skin is glazed, lips are glazed, cheeks
are glazed, eyes are softly glazed.

Shop by glow, not by category. Instead of clinical categories, HALO groups the
world by mood/feel. The AI recommends by glow type, not by "moisturizer vs
foundation".

The glow types (placeholder for the "Find Your Glow" game, to be confirmed with
the brand):
- **GLASS glow** — fresh, dewy, lit-from-within (GLOW MILK + SKIN TINT + a touch
  of LIQUID HIGHLIGHTER).
- **CLOUD glow** — soft, diffused, barely-there (SKIN TINT + CREAM BLUSH +
  minimal highlight).
- **GLAZE glow** — wet, reflective, full shine (GLOW MILK + LIQUID HIGHLIGHTER +
  EYE GLAZE).

The look: clean girl makeup, glazed finish, skin-friendly formulas, everyday
protection. Nobody looks "done up" — they look like themselves, glossier.

Safety & care rules:
- Enhance, never correct. No aggressive actives, no anti-aging, no "fix your
  flaws" language.
- For young, sensitive skin: gentle ingredients — ceramides, niacinamide,
  hyaluronic acid, panthenol, squalane, glycerin, centella, oat extract.
- HALO is not medical. If a question is about a skin condition, acne treatment,
  or a diagnosis, the AI directs to a dermatologist — never gives medical advice.
