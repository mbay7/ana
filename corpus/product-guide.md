# Product & Purchase Guide — Haven & Hearth

This guide explains the special rules that apply to particular product types.
Refer back to the Returns Policy for the general change-of-mind window.

## Furniture (large items)
- **Lead times:** many furniture pieces are made to order with a **2–4 week**
  lead time, plus delivery-day scheduling. The product page states whether an
  item is in stock or made to order.
- **Delivery:** two-person, kerbside, on a booked day (£29 flat). Room-of-choice
  placement and old-item removal are paid add-ons.
- **Assembly:** unless stated, furniture arrives flat-packed and requires
  self-assembly. An assembly service is offered at checkout for selected items.
- **Colour and fabric variations:** natural materials (wood grain, leather,
  wool) show slight variation between pieces. This variation is **not a
  defect** and is not grounds for a faulty-item return. Fault means the item
  does not function or is materially damaged.

## Personalised and made-to-order items
Personalised items (engraved, monogrammed, or made to a custom size) are
**final sale** once production has begun. Production typically begins within
24 hours of ordering, so please check your details carefully. If a personalised
item arrives faulty or incorrect versus what you ordered, your statutory rights
to a repair, replacement or refund still apply.

## Seasonal collections
- **Christmas collection** — available from October. After **1 December**, any
  unsold Christmas items become final sale and are **non-returnable**.
- **Summer garden collection** — available from April. After **1 September**,
  remaining summer garden items become final sale and are **non-returnable**.

## Hygiene-sealed items
Mattresses, mattress toppers, pillows, duvets and other bedding are sealed for
hygiene. Once the **seal is broken** (opened and removed from its sealed
packaging), the item **cannot be returned** for a change of mind, for health and
safety reasons. It can still be returned if it is faulty or not as described.

## Gift cards
Gift cards are **non-refundable**, cannot be exchanged for cash, and cannot be
used to purchase further gift cards. E-gift cards are emailed within one hour.

## Electrical and battery items
Small electricals carry a **2-year warranty** against manufacturing defects,
starting from the date of delivery. This is in addition to, and does not affect,
your statutory rights. If an electrical item develops a fault, contact support
with your order number before attempting any repair.
