# HALO — Brand

HALO is a new-generation beauty enhancement brand. Tagline: "You Glow
Differently." Supporting: "Where Beauty Becomes Play."

Category: HALO is neither traditional makeup nor clinical skincare. It is
"beauty enhancement" — a full-face glow system. 80% makeup / 20% skincare.
Makeup-first beauty with skincare support baked in.

Positioning: "Enhancement, not transformation." Bring out the natural feature
without masking it. Protect young, sensitive skin.

The four product rules:
1. Enhance — bring out the natural feature, never mask it.
2. Protect — skin-friendly formulas for young, sensitive skin (no aggressive
   actives, no anti-aging, gentle ingredients).
3. Simplify — maximum glow, minimum steps.
4. Express — room to play and personalize without looking overdone.

Core belief: "Beauty should enhance what already exists, not hide it." Girls
want to look like themselves — only fresher, softer, glossier, more confident.

Audience: Gen Alpha first, Early Gen Z as the aspirational older sister.
They discover beauty on TikTok, don't separate skincare from makeup, and want
beauty that is easy, fun, safe, and natural. They never want to "fix flaws" —
they want to enhance and glow.

HALO speaks to Gen Alpha before almost anyone else in beauty — that white space
is why it wins.
